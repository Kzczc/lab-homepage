为什么有设计感Why it feels designed

  • 轮廓先于文字：缩小到 favicon 仍能辨认四向汇聚和中心节点。The silhouette comes before the name: the four-way convergence remains legible as a favicon.
  • 一个符号承载一个概念：不是堆叠 AI、金融和图表图标，而是把“决策汇聚”作为核心记忆。One symbol carries one idea: decision convergence, rather than a pile of AI, finance, and chart icons.
  • 橙色只强调中心节点，深蓝负责学术、可信和长期使用。Orange only marks the central node; deep navy provides an academic, durable base.
  • 主标、白色版、单色版和 favicon 使用同一套几何，方便网页、论文、海报和演示统一。Primary, white, mono, and favicon versions share the same geometry across the site, papers, posters, and slides.

使用方式Usage

页眉使用白色版本；浅色背景使用双色版本；论文、代码仓库和黑白打印使用单色版本。Logo 周围保留至少一个标志高度的空白，不把标志压在复杂图片上。Use the white version in the header, the two-color version on light surfaces, and the mono version in papers, repositories, and black-and-white print. Keep at least one mark-height of clear space around it.
