大模型：学习与推理Learning & Reasoning

从数据混合与训练过程，到商业知识与模型失误，我们研究大模型如何学习、推理，以及如何被可靠地评估。From data mixing and training dynamics to business reasoning and model failures, we study how language models learn, reason, and can be evaluated reliably.

Towards Efficient LLMs Annealing with Principled Sample Selection

DiReCT 利用曲率信息选择退火阶段的训练样本，抑制横向噪声并增强沿低曲率方向的有效下降。DiReCT uses curvature information to select training samples during annealing, reducing transverse noise and strengthening descent along low-curvature directions.

PRISM: Probing Reasoning, Instruction, and Source Memory in LLM Hallucinations

PRISM 通过结构化查询分别评估源记忆、推理与指令遵循，定位模型幻觉的来源，并分析不同能力之间的优化权衡。PRISM uses structured queries to evaluate source memory, reasoning, and instruction following separately, locating the origins of hallucinations and examining trade-offs between capabilities.

智能体：协作与决策Agents & Decisions

我们研究多智能体如何检索知识、组织研究流程，并在社会与经济情境中作出判断。We study how multi-agent systems retrieve knowledge, organize research workflows, and make judgments in social and economic settings.

BizSage: A Self-Evolving Multi-Agent Framework for Business Research with Efficient Knowledge Retrieval

BizSage 通过知识图谱与章节级检索组织文献，让多个智能体协作完成商业研究任务，并从评估反馈中积累可复用的研究策略。BizSage organizes literature through knowledge graphs and section-level retrieval. Multiple agents collaborate on business research tasks and refine reusable strategies through evaluation feedback.

Mechanism Alignment for Socially Embedded Decision Making in LLMs

研究大模型在涉及他人的选择中如何处理关系与共同结果，关注个体决策机制与群体行为之间的联系。We study how language models account for relationships and collective outcomes when making choices that affect others, connecting individual decision mechanisms with group behavior.

03

金融：数据与模型Markets & Models

结合金融计量、随机过程与机器学习，研究时间序列、事件传播与市场行为。Combining financial econometrics, stochastic processes, and machine learning, we study time series, event propagation, and market behavior.

ACM ICAIF 2025ACM ICAIF 2025

LENS: Large Pre-trained Transformer for Exploring Financial Time Series Regularities

LENS 通过预训练 Transformer 学习金融时间序列规律，将可逆对比学习与时间、通道注意力结合，用于金融序列表示与预测。LENS learns financial time-series regularities with a pre-trained Transformer, combining invertible contrastive learning with time- and channel-aware attention for representation and forecasting.
