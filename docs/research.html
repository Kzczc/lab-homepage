01

大模型：学习与推理Learning & reasoning

从数据混合与训练过程，到商业知识与模型失误，我们研究大模型如何学习、推理，以及如何被可靠地评估。From data mixing and training dynamics to business reasoning and model failures, we study how language models learn, reason, and can be evaluated reliably.

DiReCT · D³

训练数据的选择与调度Training data selection and scheduling

研究如何根据优化几何与梯度关系选择、混合和排列训练数据。DiReCT 关注退火阶段的样本选择，D³ 将批次间关系建模为方向图，用于动态调度。We study how optimization geometry and gradient interactions can guide data selection, mixing, and ordering. DiReCT targets sample selection during annealing; D³ models interactions between batches to schedule training data.

PRISM · BizCompass

推理能力与模型失误的评估Evaluating reasoning and model failures

PRISM 分别诊断记忆、推理与指令遵循中的失误；BizCompass 将推理评估拓展到商业知识与应用。两项工作关注模型能做什么，以及失误发生在哪里。PRISM separates failures in source memory, reasoning, and instruction following. BizCompass extends reasoning evaluation to business knowledge and applications. Together, they examine what models can do and where they fail.

02

智能体：协作与决策Agents & decisions

我们研究多智能体如何检索知识、组织研究流程，并在社会与经济情境中作出判断。We study how multi-agent systems retrieve knowledge, organize research workflows, and make judgments in social and economic settings.

EMNLP 2026EMNLP 2026

BizSage

面向商业研究的多智能体协作Multi-agent collaboration for business research

通过知识图谱与章节级检索组织文献，让多个智能体协作完成商业研究任务，并从评估反馈中积累可复用的研究策略。BizSage organizes literature through knowledge graphs and section-level retrieval. Multiple agents collaborate on business research tasks and refine reusable research strategies through evaluation feedback.

Caught in the Story

社会情境中的判断与决策Judgment and decisions in social contexts

研究相同事实在不同叙述方式下如何影响模型判断，特别是多轮对话中的叙事俘获。相关的 Mechanism Alignment 工作进一步关注社会情境中的决策机制。We examine how different presentations of the same facts affect model judgments, including narrative captivity in multi-turn conversations. Related work on Mechanism Alignment studies decision mechanisms in social contexts.

03

金融：数据与模型Markets & models

结合金融计量、随机过程与机器学习，研究时间序列、事件传播与市场行为。Combining financial econometrics, stochastic processes, and machine learning, we study time series, event propagation, and market behavior.

LENS · HGAN-SDEs

金融时间序列与随机动态Financial time series and stochastic dynamics

LENS 通过预训练 Transformer 学习金融时间序列规律；HGAN-SDEs 以对抗训练学习随机动态。这条研究主线连接金融计量、序列建模与生成方法。LENS uses a pre-trained Transformer to learn financial time-series regularities. HGAN-SDEs learns stochastic dynamics through adversarial training, connecting financial econometrics with sequence modeling and generative methods.

FinRipple

金融事件传播与市场理解Financial events and market understanding

FinRipple 将动态公司关系、金融知识与市场反应相结合，研究事件如何在相互关联的主体间传播。相关的 FinFlier 工作关注金融文本与图表之间的知识关联。FinRipple combines changing company relationships, financial knowledge, and market reactions to model how events propagate across connected entities. Related work on FinFlier connects financial narratives with chart-based analysis.
