实验室负责人Principal Investigator

张光是香港科技大学（广州）金融科技学域助理教授，2021 年获波士顿大学经济学博士学位。他的研究连接金融科技、金融计量、机器学习与实证金融。Guang Zhang is an Assistant Professor in the Financial Technology Thrust at HKUST(GZ). He received his Ph.D. in Economics from Boston University in 2021. His research connects FinTech, financial econometrics, machine learning, and empirical finance.
