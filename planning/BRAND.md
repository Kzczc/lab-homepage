# AIDE Lab logo direction

## Why the referenced logos feel designed

OpenAI, Gemini, Claude and DeepSeek do not depend on decoration. Their marks have a compact silhouette, a clear idea, and enough contrast to survive at favicon size. They also work as a family: symbol, wordmark, single-color version, app icon, motion state, and generous spacing all share the same geometry.

The useful principles are:

- **One visual idea.** The symbol should encode a useful association without drawing a literal diagram.
- **Distinct silhouette.** A mark must be recognized in a row of small icons before its text can be read.
- **Controlled geometry.** Repeated curves, consistent terminals, and deliberate negative space create confidence.
- **Color discipline.** The mark should work in one color; a second color should only signal the key idea.
- **System behavior.** The same symbol must work in a header, favicon, paper footer, social avatar, slide title, and subtle animation.
- **Wordmark separation.** The icon should not depend on a long name baked into the image. It can sit beside `AIDE Lab`, `AI · Decisions · Economics`, or the Chinese name.

## AIDE Lab concept

The AIDE mark is a **decision convergence** symbol. Four open paths arrive from four directions and meet at a central diamond. The open paths represent evidence, models, agents, and economic context; the central node represents a decision that has to be made visible and explainable. The geometry stays abstract so it can cover future work beyond any one model or financial task.

The primary version uses the orange path system around a deep navy central diamond with an orange point. The white version is for the dark site header. The mono version is for documents, paper headers, and monochrome printing. The favicon keeps the central diamond and four directional cuts at a 32px scale.
